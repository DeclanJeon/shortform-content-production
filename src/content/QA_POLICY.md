# Automated visual QA policy — versioned narrative and visual scope

Automated QA is an observed, versioned machine assessment, never a claim of human review or perfect artistic accuracy.

## New schema3.0 plans — full storyboard + spoken-emotion-scene-v8 + image-only-storyboard-v2

Apply NARRATIVE_DELIVERY_POLICY.md and produce the complete beat→scene→shot→panel board, scene-camera intent table, paginated sheets, extracted clean panels, and split manifest before media completion. A separate story-only review must establish listener orientation, causal emotion, spoken delivery and emotional payoff before shot allocation. Retain the exact reviewed master and its execution receipt. Scene v8 treats critical reactions and functional emotional framing as material requirements, without rejecting harmless art variations. After scene review and extraction, read every actual clean panel in order without narration/synopsis/acting notes; preserve these observations. A second request compares those observations and the same panels against the story and every designated emotional turn. Both exact-plan/image-hash-bound results are required for media completion. A generic old PASS cannot substitute. Whole-sequence rejection holds the episode; it does not authorize whole-episode regeneration. Human listening and full playback remain NOT_PERFORMED unless actually exercised.

## Existing schema2.0 plans — spoken-emotion-scene-v8 + image-only-emotion-v1

Retained schema2.0 plans keep their original narrative review and media lineage. They are not silently upgraded into schema3.0 boards or re-rendered.

## Existing schema1.0 plans — narration-context-scene-v7

The following retained policy remains authoritative for existing approved/paid legacy work. New gates do not silently rewrite old approvals or cached provider outcomes.

- Character-sheet review is scoped to the supplied character sheets. Missing scene frames in this earlier stage are not a failure.
- Scene review receives a QA-only composite of the actual scene images and identity references, with explicit SCENE/REFERENCE + asset-ID panel labels, matching shot direction, and matching narration. Source image hashes and composite hash bind the assessment. Images are downscaled on the board; this is not an assertion of full-resolution inspection of every pixel. It blocks material story/action contradictions, identity drift, missing/clipped essential subjects, unreadable essential symbols, and unambiguous conflicting time cues.
- Narration is the primary causal/story contract; shot direction is secondary art direction. Harmless pose variants (e.g. leaning while listening rather than floating off the wall, without tapping/clapping) are recorded as nonblocking `observations`, not silently called exact pose conformity. Only `issues` that change narrated meaning block. Wrong identity/essential unreadability still block.
- Static images need not display literal glyphs for every narrated temporal/count concept; absence is not an explicit contradictory count. Such art-direction omissions can be retained as observations.
- Optional set dressing, decorative-dot placement, and palette warmth alone in stylized illustration are not publication blockers unless they change the narrated meaning. A 03:00 clock/night scene is stronger time evidence than cream paper or warm skin colors. This is not an exact-pixel/art-direction conformity check.
- V7 supplies the full narration as well as the matching segment, preventing an isolated line from changing the interpreted worldbuilding/function. This adds context, not a manual PASS or removal of identity/material-contradiction gates. A failed model transport is not a valid review. Diagnosed retries use a separate, bounded checkpoint and keep the original unknown checkpoint.
- Original v2/v3/v4/v5/v6 rejections remain in the audit trail. V3 fixed material-versus-decorative scope; v4 fixes scene/reference attachment-order ambiguity without relaxing material criteria. V5 distinguishes noncausal pose/art-direction departures explicitly in retained observations. V6 aligns the system-level response schema with the requested observations field and distinguishes absent static count glyphs from explicit contradictory counts. A v6 result requires a fresh assessment of the labeled actual files; it does not rewrite an old REVISE into PASS.
- Material visual defects may receive at most one paid correction per asset (two image-generation attempts total). Both original and corrected outputs and review evidence are preserved. Unknown provider outcomes are never a reason to rebuy. A second material defect stays on hold.
- Final rendered samples, full FFmpeg decode, source/plan-bound originality evidence, exact artifact checksums, and archive readback are creator-side requirements. Provider channel and processing verification belong exclusively to the separate publisher after queue handoff.
- Sampled-frame review is not whole-video human playback. Word-timestamp/caption matching is not independent human listening or ASR.
