# Spoken story and visible emotion — spoken-story-emotion-v2

Applies to newly authored creative-plan schema3.0 and explicitly requested, strictly unstarted/unpaid narrative revisions. Existing schema2.0 approvals/checkpoints remain historical versions, never rewritten or inherited as approval of different words. Paid media and known upload IDs are not regenerated by this change.

## Ordered gates
1. Lock the canonical selected pitch. Write one continuous Korean spoken story BEFORE creating shot/segment allocations. The narrator is telling a listener what happened and why it mattered, normally in a consistent third-person conversational register; do not invent personal eyewitness testimony. The text must explain relationships, mistaken expectations, choices and receiving reactions without needing the images. Repeated ~었다/~했다/~습니다 report sentences, character-name/action lists and literary summaries are not this delivery. A negative register filter blocks3 consecutive report endings or a dominant report register across6+ narrator utterances; quoted character dialogue is excluded. This only catches obvious failures, not proof of natural storytelling. Tell it like explaining a story to a friend, not a literary report, press release, or list of picture captions. Introduce people through their relevant roles, explain what they want and why a setback hurts, and connect stimulus→choice→result→changed feeling. Preserve the pitch's events/payoff and originality boundaries.250–550 non-whitespace characters is a working budget, not an excuse to omit necessary explanation. No filler or mechanical ending rotation.
2. A separate actual reviewer request sees the story, not a storyboard that could repair its omissions. Required evidence checks: `listener_orientation`, `causal_emotion`, `spoken_delivery`, `speech_register`, `emotional_payoff`. Quote actual wording and explain the effect/defect. Any failed check blocks allocation. This is text review, never a claim of actual listening or user acceptance.
3. Allocate the reviewed story into16–24 unique still shots. Joining the segments with spaces must reproduce the master, including punctuation and word spacing (only whitespace runs are normalized). Cut across connected clauses when useful; a cut need not terminate a sentence. Do not rewrite spoken words to match a convenient picture. The master object is program-owned after its review; corrections requiring a different story return upstream rather than silently patching it during allocation.
4. Each shot supplies stage/location and a performance purpose: emotion before, triggering event, ONE drawable receiving reaction, emotion after. At least two distinct critical turning points need visible evidence. Information/detail shots need not invent a facial emotion. Emotion labels are context, not acting: specify gaze, grip, distance, posture or a choice responding to another person. Identity sheets lock identity, not poses/expressions. Avoid remote callers appearing physically together before a later arrival. Functional emotional framing cannot be dismissed as decoration; keep nonessential art variation nonblocking.
5. Image prompts include actual segment words, neighboring words, story, stage and performance. First run material identity/action review. Then inspect ALL actual selected clean panels in order, paginated at readable size, without supplying narration/synopsis/acting notes. Preserve what the reviewer actually reads, including uncertainty. A separate comparison request checks those readings against the approved story, every critical emotional reaction, and whole-sequence location/reveal continuity. Narration must not manufacture a visible reaction absent from the panel. Missing critical reactions block private-media completion. Whole-sequence rejection is a hold for evidence-bound repair, not permission to rebuy the whole episode.
6. TTS uses the continuous master text, not newline-per-cut prose. Punctuation and wording must carry natural conversational breath and emphasis. For newly started fixed-template-listener-v1 media use supported Typecast tempo1.05 (previous1.2), normal preset/intensity1; preserve1.2 and exact existing inputs for previously started media. No paid re-synthesis just to slow an existing take; do not send free-form acting notes as unsupported API parameters or include them in spoken text. File hashes/captions are technical evidence, not actual listening. Human listening/full playback stays NOT_PERFORMED unless performed. Do not claim full perceptual audio/video QA from sampled frames.

## Fixed frame and listener rhythm
- Reuse the supplied hash-pinned PNG frame; do not ask the image provider to generate page UI, headers or templates. Composite scene-only art into the measured panel, retain the original brand header, replace the example title, and erase the example view count. The fixed frame is not zoomed/panned with scene art. Source bitmap and edit recipe remain in the private project archive.
- New shot performance includes structured `listening`: beat flow/expectation/reaction/payoff/closing, pause0..1.2seconds, music_mood playful/curious/warm/quiet. Require a few purposeful holds (at least2, not every shot, total<=12seconds), not uniform slow delivery. Review anticipation→answer and receiving emotion; tacked-on questions/engagement bait are not listener interaction. Old approved plans remain unchanged and lack of new directions is not fabricated model evidence.
- Match segment ends against actual provider word boundaries. Insert silence into a local derivative, never cut a word or invent timings. Shift all subsequent word captions and scene windows together, keep the receiving shot during the pause, align picture boundaries to30fps and pad less than one frame instead of cutting the final sound. The measured delivery including holds must remain<=180seconds before image spending; never accelerate/cut speech to force a limit.
- Music is selected by reviewed scene mood from a small privately cached web catalog with exact source/license/file hashes. Current candidates: Mixkit Comical, Owies Ukulele, Little Bells. Music is low-level, speech-ducked, faded and muted on quiet/serious scenes. Music synchronization permission is not permission to sell/remix standalone tracks or register Content-ID; claims remain possible. No live arbitrary web track downloads in the media worker, no asset redistribution through GitHub. Actual listening remains unverified until performed.
- Pin profile and registry per media attempt. Existing TTS/image checkpoints stay on the old path and are not resubmitted under changed inputs. Version originality review caches and current-handoff binding just like revised story plans; never reuse old approval/review evidence for new words.

## Limits and lineage
- Authorized unstarted revisions retain the exact selected pitch/cast/style and causal ending, use separate versioned text/checkpoint/history paths, supersede rather than erase the previous handoff, and require fresh narrative/content/rights review plus a new native signed production approval. Previous media admission/payment/upload evidence makes such a revision ineligible.
- Remain within15 planning requests total,2 candidates maximum,2 corrections per candidate shared between preapproval script and allocation. A narration-only revision of a locked existing story has one eligible pitch, not permission to switch stories. Actual narrative REVISE, unknown outcomes, bounded exhaustion and original rejection evidence remain authoritative; no forced PASS.
- Bind narrative review to the exact script SHA and actual request/execution receipt; bind visual sequence review to exact selected source-image hashes and plan SHA. An old generic review is not this gate's evidence.
- Preserve image maximum2 attempts per asset, existing episode/day budgets, two-hour admission cadence, archive-before-publication and same-ID publication recovery.
- Retain the spoken master and performance contracts in canonical plan JSON, editable script/storyboard and compact final QA summary. Do not archive exhaustive runtime logs.
- New QA gates do not assert audience testing, measured popularity, human approval, fresh source fetching or independent listening.
